# Demo video scripts

Two demos. Both should be **screen recordings with overlaid text or voiceover**, 45–60 seconds each. Vertical format (9:16) for WhatsApp and LinkedIn; landscape (16:9) for the website. Record both orientations once, edit twice.

Tools: QuickTime + iMovie is enough. CapCut if you want fast vertical edits.

The point of these videos is to make the buyer think *"that would work in my business"* within the first 10 seconds. No intro, no logo splash, no "Hi I'm Krishna." Just the problem and the magic.

---

## Demo 1 — 24/7 WhatsApp Lead Responder (45s)

**Setup:** Use a real WhatsApp Business sandbox number. Have a fake clinic name — "Marina Pediatric Clinic" — and a fake calendar.

| Time | Visual | Audio / Text overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | Black screen, time stamp big: **23:47** | Text: *"It's 11:47 pm. A lead just messaged your business."* |
| 0:04–0:10 | WhatsApp chat appears. Incoming message: *"Hi, are you open tomorrow? I want a check-up for my 8-year-old."* | (silent) |
| 0:10–0:15 | Cut to black for half a second. | Text: *"Your team is asleep. Most businesses lose this lead by morning."* |
| 0:15–0:30 | Back to WhatsApp. Typing indicator. AI reply appears in 4 seconds: *"Hi! Yes we're open 9 am–6 pm tomorrow. Dr. Sara has 10:30 am or 2 pm free. Which works better?"* Customer types: *"10:30"*. AI replies: *"Booked. Sending confirmation now. Anything I should let Dr. Sara know in advance?"* | (silent, just the chat playing out naturally) |
| 0:30–0:38 | Quick cut to Google Calendar — event appearing for tomorrow 10:30 with the customer's name. Then back to WhatsApp showing a confirmation card. | (silent) |
| 0:38–0:45 | Black screen. | Text: *"Lead saved. Meeting booked. You were asleep."* (pause 1s) Text small at bottom: *"AI agents for Dubai SMEs — [your domain]"* |

**Notes for recording:**
- Slow the typing down slightly so a human can read it. Real WhatsApp AI replies in 2 seconds; that's too fast for video. 4–5 seconds reads better.
- Use a real-ish customer name in the typing ("Reem K.") — adds believability.
- Background music: none, or barely-there ambient. Silence makes it feel real.

## Demo 2 — AI Voice Agent (60s)

**Setup:** Record audio of the call separately (high quality), screen-record a phone showing the incoming call + a calendar app side-by-side, then sync.

Use ElevenLabs for the AI voice — pick a warm female voice with a slight regional accent if available (sounds more believable for Dubai). Name her something simple like "Aya."

| Time | Visual | Audio / Text overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Phone ringing on a desk, nobody around. | Text: *"Your salon is busy. The phone rings. Nobody picks up."* |
| 0:05–0:08 | Phone screen shows incoming call → call connects automatically after 3 rings. | Sound of phone ringing, then click. |
| 0:08–0:14 | Audio plays over the phone screen. Subtitle on screen for each line. | **Aya:** "Hi, thanks for calling Marina Salon, this is Aya. How can I help?" |
| 0:14–0:20 | (same) | **Caller:** "Hi, do you have any openings for a haircut tomorrow afternoon?" |
| 0:20–0:30 | (same) | **Aya:** "Let me check… yes, I have 2 pm or 4:30 pm with Lina, or 3:15 pm with Maya. Which works for you?" |
| 0:30–0:35 | (same) | **Caller:** "4:30 with Lina, please." |
| 0:35–0:42 | (same) | **Aya:** "Booked, 4:30 tomorrow with Lina. Can I get your name and a number for the confirmation?" |
| 0:42–0:48 | (same) | **Caller:** "Reem, 050-XXX-XXXX." **Aya:** "Perfect, Reem. You'll get a WhatsApp confirmation in a moment. See you tomorrow." |
| 0:48–0:55 | Quick cut to calendar (new event appearing for 16:30 tomorrow, "Reem — Haircut with Lina") and a WhatsApp confirmation message popping up on a second phone. | (silent) |
| 0:55–1:00 | Black screen. | Text: *"You didn't miss the call. You also didn't pick up."* Small at bottom: *"AI agents for Dubai SMEs — [your domain]"* |

**Notes for recording:**
- This one *needs* a real-feeling voice. ElevenLabs Turbo v2.5+ is fine. Don't use the default OpenAI voice — it gives the game away in 2 seconds.
- The caller can be you, recorded with the phone held away from your face so it sounds like a regular phone call.
- Slight pauses (300–600ms) between Aya's lines and the caller's lines make it feel real. Real AI voice agents have these naturally; if your recording is too snappy, add them in edit.
- Don't show "AI" anywhere. The whole effect is "wait, that wasn't a person?" — revealed only at the end.

## Where to put them

1. **Landing page** — embed both inline, autoplay muted, loop. Voice agent goes first (more visceral).
2. **WhatsApp** — when you message the agency owner, send the vertical version as a 1-minute video. Far better than describing it.
3. **LinkedIn** — one post per video, captioned. The voice agent will out-perform the WhatsApp one on engagement — post that one first.
4. **Cold/warm outreach** — never send a pitch. Send the video and one sentence: *"Built this. Thought you'd want to see it before anyone else does."*

The videos *are* the sales process. Once they exist, you don't need to explain what you do ever again.
